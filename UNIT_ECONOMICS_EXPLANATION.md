# Unit Economics Explanation - Amazon vs ONDC

## 🎯 Your Question is Very Valid!

You're asking: **"We're comparing marketplace (Amazon) vs ONDC, but we're using brand prices (Mamaearth website). Is this correct?"**

Let me explain the logic:

---

## 📊 The Unit Economics Comparison

### **What We're Actually Comparing:**

We're comparing **profitability** for the brand when selling through:
1. **Amazon Marketplace** (platform takes commission)
2. **ONDC Network** (lower commission)

### **Key Assumption (Industry Standard):**

**D2C brands typically sell at the SAME price across all channels!**

- Product on Amazon: ₹399
- Same product on ONDC: ₹399
- Same product on brand website: ₹399

**Why?** Brands maintain consistent pricing to avoid channel conflict.

---

## 💰 The Math (Why Same Price Works)

### **Example: ₹400 Product**

**Scenario 1: Selling on Amazon**
```
Selling Price: ₹400 (what customer pays)
- Amazon Commission (18%): ₹72
- Payment Fee (2%): ₹8
- COGS (30%): ₹120
- Logistics: ₹15
- Packaging: ₹12
- CAC: ₹90
- Other Fees: ₹5
= Total Cost: ₹322
= Profit: ₹400 - ₹322 = ₹78
```

**Scenario 2: Selling on ONDC (Same Price)**
```
Selling Price: ₹400 (what customer pays - SAME!)
- ONDC Commission (6%): ₹24  ← LOWER!
- Payment Fee (2%): ₹8
- COGS (30%): ₹120
- Logistics: ₹15
- Packaging: ₹12
- CAC: ₹100 (slightly higher)
- Other Fees: ₹5
= Total Cost: ₹284
= Profit: ₹400 - ₹284 = ₹116  ← HIGHER!
```

**Result: ONDC is ₹38 more profitable per product!**

---

## 🔍 Why This Makes Sense

### **The Key Difference is COMMISSION, not Price!**

| Component | Amazon | ONDC | Impact |
|-----------|--------|------|--------|
| **Selling Price** | ₹400 | ₹400 | **Same** (brand controls) |
| **Commission** | 18% (₹72) | 6% (₹24) | **-₹48 savings** |
| **CAC** | ₹90 | ₹100 | +₹10 cost |
| **Net Benefit** | - | - | **+₹38 per product** |

**The profit improvement comes from lower commission, not different prices!**

---

## ✅ Why Using Brand Prices is Correct

### **For D2C Brands:**

1. **Price Control:** Brand sets the same price everywhere
2. **Commission Impact:** Different platforms charge different commissions
3. **Unit Economics:** We're analyzing profitability, not price comparison

### **What Matters:**
- ✅ **Commission rate difference** (18% vs 6%) = Main driver
- ✅ **Same selling price** = Standard industry practice
- ✅ **Cost structure** = Different across platforms

### **What Doesn't Matter:**
- ❌ Whether price is from Amazon or brand website
- ❌ (For this analysis, we assume same price)
