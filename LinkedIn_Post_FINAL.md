I just completed a comprehensive unit economics analysis comparing Amazon marketplace vs ONDC for a D2C brand (using Mamaearth as a case study), and the results are compelling:

📈 **Key Findings:**

✅ **Commission Rate Reduction:** Switching from Amazon (18% commission) to ONDC (6% commission) saves **12 percentage points** on every sale

✅ **Profit Per Unit Improvement:** Average profit per unit increases by **15-25%** under base case assumptions, with some products showing **50%+ improvements**

✅ **Monthly Profit Impact:** For a portfolio of 41 products, the profit improvement is significant across different price points and sales volumes

✅ **Break-Even Analysis:** Lower commission rates mean faster break-even for new product launches

For a ₹400 product:

• ONDC: ₹24 commission (6%) + other costs = ~₹284 total cost
• **Savings: ₹48 per unit** (plus direct customer data access!)

💡 **Strategic Insights:**
1. **Multi-channel approach works best:** Use Amazon for reach/discovery + ONDC for profitability
2. **Higher margin products benefit more:** Products with higher selling prices see greater absolute profit improvements
3. **Customer data ownership:** ONDC provides direct customer relationships (priceless for D2C brands)

⚠️ **Important Considerations:**
• CAC may be slightly higher on ONDC (brand building required)
• Logistics setup needed for ONDC compatibility
• Customer awareness and traffic may be lower initially
• Requires phased rollout strategy
