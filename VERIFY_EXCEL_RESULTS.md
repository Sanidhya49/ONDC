## ✅ How to Verify in Excel

### Step 1: Check the Dashboard Sheet

Look for these metrics:
- **Average Profit per Unit (ONDC)** should be **HIGHER** than Amazon
- **Average Profit Increase %** should be **POSITIVE** (15-25% expected)
- **Total Monthly Profit (ONDC)** should be **HIGHER** than Amazon

### Step 2: Check Unit_Economics Sheet

Look at column **P** (Profit_Change_pct):
- Should show **POSITIVE percentages** (like +20%, +15%, etc.)
- This means ONDC profit is higher than Amazon

### Step 3: Check the Bar Chart

In Dashboard sheet:
- The **ONDC bar** should be **taller** than Amazon bar
- This visually shows ONDC is better
